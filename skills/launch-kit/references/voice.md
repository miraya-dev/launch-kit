# Writing in the maker's voice

Every draft is written as the maker, in the first person. The goal is text the maker could post after a light edit, and that readers trust because it sounds like a person who built something, not like an ad.

## Defaults

- **First person.** "I built", "I wanted". Use "we" only for a team.
- **Plain words, short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Explain jargon once, or drop it.
- **Specific beats impressive.** Name the command, the file, the format, the limit. "Reads `package.json`, `pyproject.toml` and `Cargo.toml`" says more than "supports many ecosystems".
- **Numbers only from facts.** Use a number only if it is in the repo, in `00-facts.md` or from the user. Never estimate speed, users or savings.
- **Honest about limits.** Say what it does not do yet. Readers forgive missing features; they do not forgive surprises.
- **Ask for something specific.** "I'd love feedback on the config format" gets better replies than "Let me know what you think!"

## Match the maker

If the user shares their own writing (old posts, a blog, the README they wrote), copy its habits: sentence length, formality, humor, emoji use, British or American spelling. The README is the default sample. If English is not the maker's first language, keep the drafts simple: easier for them to edit and own.

## Words and habits to avoid

Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, game-changer, blazing fast, lightning-fast, supercharge, unleash, unlock, seamless, effortless, cutting-edge, next-gen, world-class, 10x, magic, killer, ultimate, robust, leverage, empower, elevate, delve.

Habits that read as marketing or as machine-written: "I'm thrilled/excited to announce", "In today's fast-paced world", "Here's the thing", "Say goodbye to…", chains of rhetorical questions, three adjectives in a row, an emoji at the start of every line, 🚀 in every post, ALL CAPS, exclamation marks after every sentence.

## Per channel

| Channel | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Hacker News | Technical, understated, curious. No marketing language at all. Notes only: the maker writes the final text. |
| Reddit | A member of that community talking to peers. Tailored to each subreddit's interests. |
| Product Hunt | Friendly and benefit-first for a broader audience; explain what it does before how. |
| X / Bluesky / Mastodon | One idea per post, concrete, visual. |
| LinkedIn | A short story: the problem, what you built, what you learned. Professional but personal. |
| dev.to / Hashnode | Teach something. The project is the example, not the subject of an ad. |
