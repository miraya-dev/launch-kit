# Product Hunt

Official guidance (link in the pack): [Launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) · [Sharing your launch](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch)

## Fit

Product Hunt's audience is broader than developers: makers, early adopters, product people. Developer tools and open-source projects do launch there, and do best when the value is visible in a few seconds (a UI, a clear before and after, a short video). A library with no visible output may get little attention; say so honestly and suggest whether to skip it or launch later with a demo.

## Fields and sizes

Check the live submission form before launch: limits change.

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The project name. |
| Tagline | 60 characters at most. What it does, no hype. |
| Description | Keep it to 260 characters: the form has used that limit and it is what shows in previews. |
| Launch tags | Up to 3. |
| Thumbnail | Square, 240 × 240 recommended; a GIF is allowed (its first frame may be shown as a still). |
| Gallery | 1270 × 760 recommended, at least 3 images. The first image is used as the social preview. |
| Video | Optional YouTube link; 30 to 90 seconds showing it in use. |
| Links | Repository and website. Mark it as free / open source. |
| Makers | The maker's own account; self-launching without a separate "hunter" is fine. |

## Launch day

- A launch day runs for 24 hours from 12:01 a.m. Pacific Time. Schedule the post ahead of time.
- Post the **maker's first comment** right away: who you are, why you built it, what it does, what is free and open, what is next, and one specific question. Around 150 to 250 words, friendly, no hard sell.
- Answer every comment through the day.

## Rules about votes

Product Hunt's own guidance says to **ask for support or feedback, not upvotes**. Asking specifically for upvotes, mass-messaging people, offering rewards for votes, paid votes and vote exchanges break its policies and can get the launch removed or the account suspended. Telling your own audience "we're live on Product Hunt, I'd love your feedback" is fine.

Never claim a badge or rank ("#1 Product of the Day") the launch has not earned.

## In the pack

1. A fit check (go, wait or skip) with the reason.
2. Name, tagline (`limit=60`, with two alternatives), description (`limit=260`), three launch tags.
3. The maker's first comment.
4. A thumbnail and gallery brief: one line per image saying what it shows and the caption.
5. A short launch-day checklist.
